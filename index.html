Printing Techniques

Eleven printing methods, one production floor

From everyday plastisol runs to specialty foil and glow builds, every technique below is printed, cured and inspected in-house — never subcontracted to a second facility.

Plastisol Printing

Our most requested method — opaque, durable and consistent across large production runs, with sharp edges on fine detail and small type.

Best for: Bulk apparel drops

Water Based Printing

A soft, breathable finish that sinks into the fabric rather than sitting on top of it — favoured by fashion and streetwear labels chasing a vintage hand-feel.

Best for: Soft-hand streetwear tees

Discharge Printing

Removes the garment's original dye and replaces it with pigment, producing an ultra-soft, almost invisible print on dark cotton fabrics.

Best for: Dark garment, soft print

High Density Printing

Ink is built up in raised, sharply defined layers for a sculpted, embroidery-like texture without the weight or cost of embroidery.

Best for: Premium logo branding

Puff Printing

A foaming additive lifts the ink off the surface for a rounded, three-dimensional effect that reads instantly on hoodies and heavyweight fleece.

Best for: Hoodies & streetwear

Metallic Printing

Fine metal pigment suspended in ink for a subtle gold, silver or bronze shimmer that holds its shine through repeated washing.

Best for: Luxury & occasion wear

Reflective Printing

High-visibility ink that activates under direct light, engineered for outdoor, tactical and performance apparel that needs to be seen after dark.

Best for: Outdoor & performance gear

Glow Printing

Phosphorescent ink that charges under light and emits a visible glow in the dark — a signature detail for youth, streetwear and event apparel.

Best for: Statement graphic tees

Foil Printing

A heat-applied metallic foil bonded over an adhesive base for a mirror-bright, high-impact finish on logos and small graphic elements.

Best for: Premium capsule collections

Vintage Printing

A deliberately cracked, faded finish achieved through controlled ink dilution and curing — built to look decades old on day one.

Best for: Retro & heritage branding

Silicone Printing

A flexible, rubber-like print with exceptional stretch recovery, formulated for performance fabrics and activewear that moves with the body.

Best for: Activewear & sportswear
Manufacturing Process

Ten checkpoints between your artwork and your shipment

Every order — from a single sample to a full production run — moves through the same disciplined sequence, so quality never depends on who happens to be on the floor that day.

01

Artwork Review

Files are checked for resolution, colour count and print feasibility before anything touches a screen.

02

Color Separation

Designs are separated into individual colour channels and mapped to exact Pantone references.

03

Screen Preparation

Mesh screens are coated, exposed and washed out to hold fine detail across the full run.

04

Ink Mixing

Inks are hand-mixed in-house and matched against your approved Pantone before a single print runs.

05

Sample Approval

A physical strike-off is printed and sent for your sign-off before bulk production begins.

06

Production

Approved artwork moves to the print floor, run on calibrated presses under consistent conditions.

07

Heat Curing

Every print passes through temperature-controlled dryers to lock ink adhesion and wash durability.

08

Inspection

Garments are checked piece by piece for registration, saturation and stitching before packing.

09

Packing

Finished goods are folded, polybagged and cartoned to your specification, ready for retail or FBA.

10

Shipping

Cartons are documented and released to your forwarder or shipped directly to your warehouse.

Quality Control

Nothing ships without clearing every checkpoint

Our inspection line works from a written checklist on every order, not a sample pull at the end. If a unit fails any one of these checks, it's pulled before it reaches your carton.

  • Color & Pantone Accuracy

    Printed colour is checked against your approved strike-off under standardised lighting.

  • Print Registration

    Multi-colour designs are checked for alignment with zero visible ghosting or offset.

  • Wash & Rub Durability

    Sample units are tested for cracking, fading and adhesion failure before bulk approval.

  • Fabric & Stitch Integrity

    Seams, hems and fabric weight are checked against your original tech pack.

  • Measurement & Fit Audit

    Random units are pulled from every size run and measured against your size chart tolerance.

  • Packaging Compliance

    Folding, polybagging and carton labelling are verified against your packing instructions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything brands ask before their first production run

What is the minimum order quantity for screen printing?

Our standard MOQ for private label programs starts at 300 pieces per style/colourway. Lower quantities can be discussed for repeat clients or brands validating a new design before scaling.

Can you match my brand's exact Pantone colors?

Yes. Every order is mixed against your submitted Pantone reference and confirmed with a physical strike-off sample before bulk ink is prepared, so your brand colour stays consistent order after order.

Do you offer OEM, ODM and private label manufacturing?

We operate all three models. OEM covers production of your existing, finished designs. ODM covers development from a concept, sketch or reference. Private label adds your branding, neck labels and packaging to a fully custom program.

Which screen printing technique is right for my product?

It depends on fabric, budget and finish. Plastisol suits bulk basics, water based and discharge suit soft-hand fashion pieces, and puff, foil or high density suit statement logo placements. Our team will recommend a technique once we see your design and fabric.

How long does a full production run take?

Standard production timelines run 15–30 days after sample approval, depending on order volume, fabric sourcing and the number of print locations. Rush timelines can be discussed at quoting stage.

Do you provide physical samples before bulk production?

Yes. Every order includes a pre-production sample or strike-off for your written approval. Bulk production only begins once you've signed off on colour, placement and print quality.

Can you print on fabrics I supply myself?

We can work with client-supplied fabric on a case-by-case basis, though most partners find it more efficient to source through our existing fabric network, which is already calibrated to our printing and curing processes.

What file formats do you accept for artwork?

Vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) are preferred for the cleanest colour separation. High-resolution PNG or PSD files are also accepted, though vector artwork typically produces sharper screens.

Can you handle multi-location printing on a single garment?

Yes. Front, back, sleeve, hem and inside-neck placements can be registered and cured within a single coordinated production pass, quoted together as part of your original order.

Do you offer custom packaging and labeling?

Private label orders can include custom neck labels, hang tags, polybags and carton branding, specified during the development stage alongside your artwork and fabric.

Which countries do you currently ship to?

We regularly ship finished goods to partners across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Australia, with freight and documentation support handled on our end.

Can BLACKFORGEX handle Amazon FBA prep requirements?

Yes. We can fold, polybag, label and carton finished goods to FBA specifications, with barcode placement and carton labelling confirmed against your Amazon requirements before shipment.

What happens if a batch doesn't meet quality standards?

Units that fail inspection are pulled and reworked or reprinted before packing. Our QC checklist runs on every unit, not a sample pull, so issues are caught before a carton is sealed, not after it arrives with you.

Do you require exclusivity or long-term contracts?

No. We work order by order. Many partners scale into ongoing production schedules over time, but there's no exclusivity requirement to place an initial order with us.

How do I get started with BLACKFORGEX?

Send your artwork, fabric preference and target quantity through our quote request form or WhatsApp. Our team will respond with technique recommendations, pricing and a timeline within one business day.
