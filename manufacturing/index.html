The Process

From Raw Material to Export Pallet

Six controlled stages, all in-house, all documented.

01 · Specification & Development

We start from your hazard, standard and volume. Our technical team translates the brief into a buildable specification and material plan.

02 · Material Selection

Hides are tan-selected for temper; yarns are chosen by fibre, gauge and blend. Every input is matched to the target performance.

03 · Knitting & Cutting

Seamless liners are knitted on 10–18 gauge machines; leather is precision-cut to nested patterns for consistency and yield.

04 · Coating & Assembly

Automated dipping lines apply nitrile, latex, PU and PVC at controlled weight; sewing lines assemble with Kevlar® or bonded thread.

05 · Inspection & Testing

Our QC lab runs abrasion, cut (TDM-100), tear, puncture and heat tests. Every batch is checked to a documented AQL against its rated spec.

06 · Packing & Export

Gloves are labelled, private-label packed, cartoned and loaded — with CE, test reports and REACH documentation for clean customs clearance.

Quality Control Laboratory

Every Batch, Tested to Its Rating

A safety rating is only as good as the testing behind it. Our on-site laboratory verifies every production batch against its stated specification before it ships.

  • EN 388 mechanical — abrasion, cut (coup & TDM-100), tear and puncture
  • EN 407 heat — contact, convective and radiant heat factors
  • EN 511 cold — convective and contact cold, water penetration
  • Dimensional & AQL — sizing, construction and defect sampling per lot
