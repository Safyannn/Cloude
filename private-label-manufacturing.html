Private Label Production Built to Launch and Scale a Brand, Not Just Fill an Order

Whether you're launching your first collection or moving an established brand's production to a more capable factory, private label manufacturing only works if the details are handled with the same care as the garment itself. That means your woven labels, hangtags and packaging need to match your brand as precisely as your stitching does. We treat labeling and branding as part of production, not an afterthought bolted on at the end.

Woven Labels

A woven label is often the first thing a customer touches when they check the size or care instructions on a garment — it's a small detail that signals whether a brand is established or still figuring itself out. We produce woven labels in a range of finishes, from clean damask weaves for everyday branding to satin-finish weaves for a softer, more premium hand-feel on lingerie, baby wear or formal pieces.

Custom Hangtags

A hangtag is the first impression a product makes before it's even worn. We produce hangtags in the paper stock, print finish and shape your brand needs — from simple kraft-card tags for a minimal, sustainable look to foil-stamped or embossed tags for a premium retail shelf presence.

Get a Quote

Heat Transfer Labels

For brands that want the clean look of a printed label without the texture or cost of a woven one, heat transfer labels bond directly to the fabric for a smooth, tag-free finish — a common choice for activewear and technical apparel where a stitched label could cause irritation against skin.

Get a Quote

Printed Satin & Care Labels

Satin labels hold fine print detail exceptionally well, which makes them the standard choice for care and content labels that need to stay legible — fiber content, wash instructions, country of origin — without adding bulk to a garment's inside seam.
