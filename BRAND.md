## What the business is

OEM, ODM and private label apparel manufacturer specializing in premium screen
printing. Runs its own printing floor, ink lab and quality line in Sialkot.

Claims the site makes (safe to repeat, attribute to BlackForgeX):

- 18+ years in manufacturing; ships to partners in 40+ countries
- 11 in-house printing techniques: plastisol, water based, discharge, high
  density, puff, metallic, reflective, glow, foil, vintage, silicone
- Standard MOQ: 300 pieces per style/colourway (lower for repeat clients or
  design validation, by discussion)
- Production: 15-30 days after sample approval
- Physical pre-production sample or strike-off on every order
- Fabrics printed: cotton, French terry, fleece, poly cotton, performance
  fabric, polyester, softshell, canvas
- Services: screen printing, fabric sourcing, private label program
  (neck labels, hang tags, polybags, carton branding), embroidery, FBA prep
- Order by order, no exclusivity or long-term contract required

Claims on the live product pages (blackforgex.com/ski-wear-manufacturer/,
/hardshell-jacket-manufacturer/):

- Ski jackets, ski pants, bibs, vests, shell and insulated jackets, mid-layers
- Base layers: thermal, compression and merino (blackforgex.com/base-layers-manufacturer/)
- 2.5-layer and 3-layer waterproof fabrics, membrane-laminated ripstop
- In-house pressure-taped seam sealing (not subcontracted)
- 50,000+ sq ft facility in Sialkot; fabric sourcing, pattern, cut, sew, finish

**Inconsistent numbers, do not quote until reconciled:** the landing page says
18+ years and 40+ countries; the ski/hardshell pages say 20+ years, 500+ staff
and 30+ countries.

## What the business is NOT (never imply these)

- Not a membrane brand: it sources membranes and fabrics, it does not make them.
- Not a broker or sourcing agent.
- Not a retailer or consumer brand.

## Audience

- **Primary:** founders and product leads starting or scaling an apparel brand
  (streetwear, sportswear, outdoor, corporate/uniform), often first-time buyers
  of manufacturing.
- **Expertise:** beginner to intermediate on manufacturing; strong on brand.
- **Problems they are solving:** finding a reliable factory, MOQs, colour
  consistency, lead times, sampling, cost per unit, quality control.
- **Misconceptions:** that design is the hard part (the site argues factory
  reliability is); that one print method works on every fabric (the site says
  softshell needs a different ink/cure than cotton).

## Point of view

"Most brands don't fail because of a bad design. They fail because of a
factory that can't hold a Pantone, misses a ship date, or disappears once the
deposit clears." Content should be practical, manufacturing-literate, and
honest about risk and cost.

## Editorial rules

**Do**
- Cite primary sources (regulators, standards bodies, industry associations,
  public company filings) for any statistic.
- Give concrete numbers a founder can plan with (MOQs, timelines, specs) and
  say where they come from.
- Explain trade-offs, not just best practices.

**Don't**
- No fabricated statistics, testimonials, or case studies.
- No hard-sell copy in the body; at most one brand mention, near the end.
- No claims about capabilities the landing page does not state.

**Taboo phrases:** "game-changer", "revolutionize", "in today's fast-paced
world", "unlock", "elevate your brand".

## Voice

Direct, confident, plain English. Short declarative sentences mixed with
longer explanatory ones. Second person ("you") for the reader, "we" only for
BlackForgeX itself. British/US spelling: the site uses British spelling
("colour") in places; blog posts use US spelling for a US-majority search
audience.
