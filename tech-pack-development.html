Why It Matters

The Tech Pack Is the Single Most Important Document in Apparel Production

Every costly production mistake — the wrong fit, the wrong seam, the wrong fabric weight — traces back to a tech pack that was unclear or missing entirely. A tech pack is the blueprint a factory follows: it removes guesswork, cuts down on sampling rounds, and protects you from paying for a bulk run that doesn't match your vision. Whether you're a first-time founder with a rough sketch or an established brand formalizing an existing product, a proper tech pack is where dependable production starts.

What's In a BlackForgeX Tech Pack

Everything a Factory Needs, Nothing It Has to Guess

A complete tech pack removes the back-and-forth that slows production. Every BlackForgeX tech pack includes:

Technical flat sketches (front, back, and detail views)
Full graded measurement specifications across your size range
Bill of materials — every fabric, trim, thread and component
Construction and stitching details, seam by seam
Fabric specifications including weight, composition and finish
Color references and colorways
Label, hangtag and packaging placement
Print, embroidery or branding placement details
Special construction notes and callouts

Professional Tech Pack Services for Apparel Brands

A tech pack is a detailed specification document that tells a manufacturer exactly how to produce your garment — measurements, materials, construction methods, colors and finishing, all in one place. Without it, a factory is left interpreting your intent, which is how fit problems, wrong fabrics and inconsistent bulk runs happen.

BlackForgeX creates factory-ready tech packs for technical, outdoor and performance apparel — the categories where construction detail matters most and where a generic apparel tech pack falls short.

Built by a Manufacturer, Not Just a Design Studio

Many tech pack providers are design studios that have never run a production line. The gap between a nice-looking tech pack and a truly production-ready one shows up on the factory floor — in details that a designer might not know a machine operator needs.

Our tech packs are built with production in mind from the first sketch, because the same company documenting your product is the one capable of manufacturing it. That connection between documentation and production is what keeps errors low and sampling rounds few.

A Tech Pack Is an Investment That Pays for Itself

Brands sometimes try to skip the tech pack to save money upfront — and then pay far more in extra sampling rounds, production errors and delayed launches. A precise tech pack is one of the cheapest ways to protect a much larger production investment.

Whether you need a single tech pack for one product or documentation across an entire collection, we scale the work to your stage — from a first-time launch to an established brand standardizing its catalog.
