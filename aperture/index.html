The photographer 02 — Studio

Behind Aperture is Elias Rome — a photographer who believes the best images aren't taken, they're waited for.

For over a decade, the studio has photographed weddings, families, and editorial stories across three continents — always with the same small, quiet approach. No shot lists that flatten a day into a checklist. No poses that don't come from the people in front of the lens. Just attention, patience, and a trained eye for the second before and the second after the one everyone expects.

Every commission is shot on a mix of digital and film, edited by hand, and delivered as work made to outlive the trend it was taken in.
