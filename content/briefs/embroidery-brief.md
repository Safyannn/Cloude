## SECTION 9 — TEXT BLOCK 2 (SEO)

**H2:** Why the Right Backing and Thread Matter as Much as the Design

**Paragraph 1:** The same logo needs different handling on different fabrics. A dense design on a lightweight performance fabric needs the correct stabilizer to prevent puckering; a cap needs different tensioning than a flat panel. Getting these choices right is the difference between embroidery that looks professional and embroidery that distorts after the first wash.

**Paragraph 2:** We match backing, stabilizer, thread type and stitch density to your specific fabric and placement — a level of control that comes from embroidering within our own manufacturing line rather than sending the work to a third-party decorator.

## SECTION 10 — TEXT BLOCK 3 (SEO)

**H2:** One Note on Color Matching for Embroidery

**Paragraph 1:** Unlike screen printing or sublimation, where we can mix inks and dyes to hit a precise Pantone reference, embroidery colors are limited to the stock range offered by thread suppliers — threads can't be custom-mixed the way ink can. We'll match your brand colors to the closest available thread shades and confirm them with you before production, so there are no surprises.

**Paragraph 2:** If exact color matching across a large brand program is critical, we'll talk you through where embroidery, printing or a combination of both makes the most sense for each part of your product.

## SUGGESTED FAQ (ADD 6–8 FOR SEO/AI-SEARCH VALUE — SEE MASTER PROMPT'S GEO SECTION)

1. **Do you digitize artwork, or do I need to provide a digitized file?**
We digitize your artwork in-house — you can send a standard logo file and we'll convert it into a production-ready embroidery file suited to your garment and placement.

2. **What garments can you embroider?**
Caps, polos, workwear, jackets, bags and most woven and knit apparel. Very lightweight or highly stretchy fabrics need the correct backing, which we select per fabric.

3. **What's the difference between flat and 3D puff embroidery?**
Flat embroidery sits flush against the fabric — standard for most logos. 3D puff embroidery uses foam under the stitching to raise the design, most common on structured caps.

4. **Can you color-match my exact brand colors in embroidery?**
Embroidery threads come in a fixed stock range and can't be custom-mixed like ink. We match your brand colors to the closest available thread shades and confirm them before production.

5. **Can you combine embroidery and printing on the same garment?**
Yes — because we run embroidery, screen printing and sublimation in-house, we can combine methods on a single garment within one production timeline.

6. **Do you produce embroidered patches?**
Yes — custom embroidered patches in any shape or size, which we can also apply to your garments.

7. **What is the minimum order quantity for embroidery?**
MOQs vary by garment and embroidery type. Share your product and quantity target and we'll confirm exact minimums.
