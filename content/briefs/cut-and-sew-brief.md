## SUGGESTED FAQ (ADD 6–8 FOR SEO/AI-SEARCH VALUE — SEE MASTER PROMPT'S GEO SECTION)

1. **What's the difference between cut and sew manufacturing and blank garment decoration?**
Cut and sew starts from raw fabric — cutting custom patterns and sewing garments from scratch to your specifications. Blank garment decoration adds prints or embroidery to an already-manufactured garment.

2. **Can you cut and sew technical fabrics like waterproof membranes and laminates?**
Yes — our cutting and sewing lines are calibrated for membranes, laminates, insulation and other heavyweight technical fabrics, not just basic cotton.

3. **What is the minimum order quantity for cut and sew production?**
MOQs vary by garment complexity and fabric type. Share your target product and quantity and we'll confirm exact minimums.

4. **Do you provide a sample before bulk production?**
Yes — every order moves into bulk production only after the sample is reviewed and approved.

5. **Can you handle complex constructions like taped seams and multi-panel patterns?**
Yes, this is a core part of our cut and sew capability for tactical, workwear and technical outdoor apparel.

6. **Where is BlackForgeX's cut and sew production based?**
Our facility is based in Sialkot, Pakistan, handling cutting, sewing, finishing and inspection fully in-house.

7. **How is quality maintained across large production runs?**
Every order is inspected with the same standard regardless of size, so a 200-unit run and a 5,000-unit run receive the same level of quality control.
