## SECTION 3 — LABEL TYPE CARDS (4 cards, image + heading + description + CTA)

### Card 1 — Woven Labels
A woven label is often the first thing a customer touches when they check the size or care instructions on a garment — it's a small detail that signals whether a brand is established or still figuring itself out. We produce woven labels in a range of finishes, from clean damask weaves for everyday branding to satin-finish weaves for a softer, more premium hand-feel on lingerie, baby wear or formal pieces.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 2 — Custom Hangtags
A hangtag is the first impression a product makes before it's even worn. We produce hangtags in the paper stock, print finish and shape your brand needs — from simple kraft-card tags for a minimal, sustainable look to foil-stamped or embossed tags for a premium retail shelf presence.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 3 — Heat Transfer Labels
For brands that want the clean look of a printed label without the texture or cost of a woven one, heat transfer labels bond directly to the fabric for a smooth, tag-free finish — a common choice for activewear and technical apparel where a stitched label could cause irritation against skin.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 4 — Printed Satin & Care Labels
Satin labels hold fine print detail exceptionally well, which makes them the standard choice for care and content labels that need to stay legible — fiber content, wash instructions, country of origin — without adding bulk to a garment's inside seam.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

## SUGGESTED FAQ (ADD 6–8 FOR SEO/AI-SEARCH VALUE — SEE MASTER PROMPT'S GEO SECTION)

1. **What's the difference between private label and white label manufacturing?**
White label uses a proven, ready-made product design that you brand as your own. Private label starts from your own specifications — fabric, construction, fit — built from scratch for your brand. We offer both.

2. **What is the minimum order quantity for private label production?**
MOQs vary by product and label type. Share your target garment and quantity and we'll confirm exact minimums for your specific program.

3. **Can you produce woven labels, hangtags and packaging together with the garment?**
Yes — labeling, hangtags and packaging are produced as part of the same production timeline as the garment, not sourced separately.

4. **Do you offer heat transfer labels for activewear and technical apparel?**
Yes. Heat transfer labels are a common choice for performance and technical apparel where a sewn-in label could cause irritation against the skin.

5. **Can I start with a small private label run as a new brand?**
Yes — we work with both first-time brand owners and established labels, and can guide you through realistic MOQs and sample development for a first-time launch.

6. **Do you handle both design guidance and bulk production?**
Yes. Our team supports you from initial concept and sample approval through to bulk production and shipping, with one point of contact throughout.
