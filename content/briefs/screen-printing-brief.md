## SECTION 3 — METHOD CARDS (6 cards, image + heading + description + CTA)

### Card 1 — Plastisol Screen Printing
Our standard, most-requested print method. Plastisol sits on top of the fabric with strong opacity, which means bold colors and clean logos show up clearly even on black or navy garments. It's durable, consistent across large runs, and the default choice for corporate branding, workwear and everyday apparel.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 2 — Water-Based Screen Printing
Water-based ink soaks into the fibers instead of sitting on top, so the final print has almost no texture — you feel the fabric, not the ink. This is the method we recommend for performance tees and lightweight technical layers, where a soft hand-feel matters more than maximum opacity.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 3 — Puff Print
Foaming agents in the ink lift the print off the surface as it cures, creating a raised, tactile texture that catches the eye. Works best on heavier fabrics like hoodies, fleece and outerwear, where a flat print would get lost.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 4 — Discharge Print
Instead of adding a layer of ink, discharge printing removes the garment's original dye and replaces it with color — the result is a soft, slightly faded finish with zero texture, closer to how a vintage tee looks after years of washing. Works only on cotton and cotton-heavy blends.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 5 — Reflective & Safety Print
Micro-glass beads embedded directly into the ink reflect light back at its source, making this print method genuinely functional, not just decorative. Built for tactical gear, workwear and low-visibility outdoor apparel where safety compliance actually matters — tested for durability through repeated washing.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

### Card 6 — Foil Print
A metallic foil layer bonded to the garment under heat and pressure, used for sharp, reflective branding accents on premium or limited-run collections. A finishing touch, not a full-coverage print method.
**CTA:** Get a Quote

## SUGGESTED FAQ (ADD 8–10 FOR SEO/AI-SEARCH VALUE — SEE MASTER PROMPT'S GEO SECTION)

1. **What is the minimum order quantity for screen printing?**
MOQs vary by method and garment type — plastisol and water-based generally allow lower minimums than foil or reflective printing. Share your product and target quantity and we'll confirm exact minimums.

2. **Can you match my brand's exact Pantone colors?**
Yes. Every order is matched against Pantone reference codes and a physical strike-off sample is approved before full production begins.

3. **Do you print on waterproof or laminated technical fabrics?**
Yes — ink type, mesh count and cure settings are adjusted specifically for membranes, laminates and heavyweight blends that standard print shops aren't set up to handle.

4. **What's the difference between plastisol and water-based printing?**
Plastisol sits on top of the fabric with strong opacity and durability. Water-based ink soaks into the fibers for a softer feel, with slightly less opacity on dark garments.

5. **Can you handle prints in multiple locations on one garment?**
Yes — chest, back, sleeve and hem prints can be planned and produced in a single coordinated run, with placement confirmed before production starts.

6. **Do you support private label and OEM printing programs?**
Yes, private label and OEM production is core to our business, including custom labels, hangtags and packaging alongside printing.

7. **Can you print reflective designs for tactical or workwear brands?**
Yes. Our reflective printing is tested for wash durability, suited to tactical gear, workwear and low-visibility outdoor apparel.

8. **Do you provide a sample before bulk production?**
Yes — a sample is produced and approved before we move into bulk production on every order, regardless of quantity.
