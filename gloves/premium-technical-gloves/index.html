Overview

Precision-Manufactured Premium Technical Gloves

The premium technical range is where every capability in the factory converges. These are the flagship gloves — multi-hazard constructions that fuse high cut resistance, back-of-hand impact protection, precision grip and genuine all-day comfort into a single refined product for premium safety brands and the most demanding end-users.

We reserve our finest materials and most advanced construction for this tier: 18-gauge engineered composite liners, micro-foam and nano-grip palm coatings, seamless anatomical patterning, laser-cut touchscreen fingertips and moulded low-profile impact protection. Every premium model is a candidate for full OEM/ODM development — this is the range partners build their signature products on.

Materials & Construction

Built From the Yarn Up

We control construction at every stage — material selection, knitting or cutting, coating or sewing, and inspection — so performance is engineered in, not hoped for.

  • 18-gauge engineered HPPE / composite high-cut liners
  • Micro-foam nitrile and nano-textured grip coatings
  • Low-profile moulded TPR impact protection

FAQ

Premium Technical Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying premium technical gloves at volume.

Materials, construction and finishing. Premium models use our finest engineered yarns, advanced low-bulk coatings, seamless anatomical patterning and refined finishing — bound cuffs, laser-cut touchscreen tips, custom labelling. The result is multi-hazard protection that feels closer to a bare hand and lasts longer, at a specification premium buyers will pay for.
Yes — this is the range built for it. Our ODM team develops signature gloves to your performance targets, aesthetic and branding, through a structured sample-and-approval cycle. Bespoke development starts at 3,000 pairs, which supports the custom patterns, moulds and labels involved.
Through material engineering, not layering. An 18-gauge composite liner delivers A5–A6 cut at a thickness that still articulates, micro-foam coatings grip without bulk, and low-profile moulded TPR adds impact protection without stiffness. The skill is achieving the protection matrix while keeping the glove thin and flexible.
We start from your performance brief and aesthetic direction, produce initial samples for fit and function, iterate against your feedback, then lock an approved pre-production sample as the golden standard for bulk. Custom labels, colours, moulded branding and packaging are all developed in parallel. Typical development runs 4–8 weeks before bulk production begins.
