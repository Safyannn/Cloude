Overview

Precision-Manufactured Specialized Industrial Gloves

Some environments impose requirements a general work glove cannot meet, and this category is where Heliotrope Safety's technical and compliance capability shows. We manufacture chemical-resistant gloves tested to EN 374, food-contact gloves compliant with EU 10/2011, anti-static/ESD gloves for electronics, and controlled-particulate gloves for clean handling — each engineered to its regulatory framework, not adapted from a generic base.

Because these applications carry real compliance risk, documentation is part of the product. We supply permeation data, food-contact declarations, surface-resistance test results and REACH conformity with every relevant shipment, so your own quality, HACCP and audit teams have the evidence they need on file from day one.

ESD gloves incorporate carbon-conductive yarn that controls surface resistance (typically below 10⁸ ohms, to EN 16350), safely dissipating static that would otherwise damage sensitive electronic components.
