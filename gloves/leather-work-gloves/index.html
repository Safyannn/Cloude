Overview

Precision-Manufactured Leather Work Gloves

Leather remains the workhorse of hand protection, and Heliotrope Safety produces the full range in-house: driver gloves, rigger gloves, split-leather palm gloves and reinforced double-palm designs. We tan-select every hide lot for consistent thickness and temper, then cut, sew and inspect to a documented AQL so a 10,000-pair order performs like the approved sample.

Our leather program spans full-grain cowhide for abrasion life, soft goatskin for dexterity-critical tasks, and economical split leather for high-turnover environments. Keystone thumbs, Kevlar-stitched seams and reinforced palm patches are standard construction options, and every model is tested for EN 388 abrasion, cut, tear and puncture before it ships.

Materials & Construction

Built From the Yarn Up

We control construction at every stage — material selection, knitting or cutting, coating or sewing, and inspection — so performance is engineered in, not hoped for.

  • Full-grain cowhide (1.1–1.3 mm) for maximum abrasion and tear resistance
  • Grade-A goatskin for high dexterity and tensile strength
  • Split cowhide (side and shoulder split) for economical high-volume programs
  • Pigskin options for breathability and stay-soft-after-wet performance
  • Kevlar® stitched seams, cotton drill backs and rubberised safety cuffs

FAQ

Leather Work Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying leather work gloves at volume.

Grain leather is the smooth outer hide layer — denser, more abrasion-resistant and water-tolerant, ideal for drivers and precision handling. Split leather is the inner fibrous layer — more economical and breathable, used for riggers and general site gloves. We stock both and will recommend the grade that matches your task and budget.
Yes. On OEM runs we dye leather to your Pantone reference and build any cuff configuration — safety, gauntlet, band-top or knit-wrist. Colour-matched pre-production samples are approved before bulk cutting begins.
All leather is tanned and tested to EN ISO 17075 for chromium-VI below the regulated limit, with REACH declarations supplied per shipment so your EU customs clearance is straightforward.
