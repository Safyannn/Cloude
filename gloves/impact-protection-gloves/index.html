Overview

Precision-Manufactured Impact Protection Gloves

Crush and pinch injuries to the back of the hand are among the most common — and most preventable — incidents in heavy industry. Heliotrope Safety's impact range integrates moulded thermoplastic rubber (TPR) protectors across the fingers, knuckles and metacarpals, tested to the EN 388:2016 impact requirement and marked with the level P designation.

We build impact protection onto the platform the job needs: cut-resistant liners for oilfield and metal work, waterproof shells for offshore, and anti-vibration palm padding for percussive tools. TPR is engineered for low-temperature flexibility so protection does not turn rigid on a cold rig, and every mould is designed to flex with the hand rather than fight it.

Impact Protection Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying impact protection gloves at volume.

The 2016 revision of EN 388 added an optional impact test. A glove that passes is marked with a 'P' as the sixth character of its rating. It confirms the back-of-hand protectors transmit below a defined force threshold under a standardised impact — real, measured crush protection, not a marketing claim.
Yes, and it commonly is in oil and gas. We fuse a moulded TPR back onto an HPPE cut liner so a single glove handles both the pinch hazard of pipe and tool work and the laceration hazard of sharp edges. Specify the cut level you need and we build the liner to match.
Standard TPR stiffens in the cold; our cold-flexible compound retains impact absorption and articulation down to sub-zero rig and mine temperatures. For extreme cold we pair it with insulated, waterproof shells.
Yes. Above 3,000 pairs we tool a custom mould so your brand or safety marking is raised directly into the back-of-hand protector — the most visible surface of a work glove.
