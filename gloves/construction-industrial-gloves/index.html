Overview

Precision-Manufactured Construction & Industrial Gloves

Construction consumes hand protection faster than almost any sector, and it needs gloves that balance protection, grip and cost across a huge headcount. Heliotrope Safety's construction and industrial range is built for that reality: durable nitrile- and latex-coated knits for concrete and block work, reinforced leather for rebar and steel, and hi-vis models for roadwork and infrastructure.

We engineer these gloves around abrasion life and grip on rough, wet and dusty surfaces — the everyday enemies of a site glove. Reinforced palms, sandy-nitrile textures and breathable backs keep crews protected and comfortable through long shifts, and standardised sizing across the range makes multi-model site programs simple to manage and reorder.

FAQ

Construction & Industrial Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying construction & industrial gloves at volume.

Crinkle-latex palms give the best wet grip on concrete, block and wet aggregate and resist the abrasion of rough surfaces. For oily or dusty handling, sandy-nitrile performs better. Many sites run both — latex for wet trades, nitrile for dry — and we supply a matched program.
Yes. Any of our coated construction gloves can be built on an HPPE cut-resistant liner instead of cotton/poly, raising protection to ANSI A4/A5 for crews handling sheet metal, glass, blades or banding while keeping the same familiar coated-palm format.
Yes. Site gloves are commonly branded with a contractor's name for asset control and safety visibility. We screen-print palms and backs, and add hi-vis and reflective elements. Custom branding is viable from 3,000 pairs.
We use a single standardised size-grading system across the construction range, so a size 10 in a latex glove fits the same hand as a size 10 in leather. That makes multi-model site programs far easier to order, issue and reorder.
