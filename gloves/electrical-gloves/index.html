Overview

Precision-Manufactured Electrical Gloves

Electrical hand protection is life-safety equipment, and it is governed by some of the strictest standards in the industry. Heliotrope Safety supplies insulating rubber gloves manufactured and tested to EN 60903 / IEC 60903 across voltage classes 00 to 4, together with the leather over-protectors and arc-flash gloves that complete a compliant electrical PPE system.

Every insulating glove is dielectrically tested and individually marked with its class, test voltage and date, and we support the retest and inspection regime these gloves require in service. For arc-flash exposure we supply gloves rated to the relevant ATPV, and we advise on the correct layered system — inner insulating glove plus outer mechanical protector — for each voltage and task class.

FAQ

Electrical Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying electrical gloves at volume.

The classes define maximum AC working voltage: Class 00 = 500 V, Class 0 = 1,000 V, Class 1 = 7,500 V, Class 2 = 17,000 V, Class 3 = 26,500 V, Class 4 = 36,000 V. Each is factory dielectric-tested well above its working voltage and marked with class, test voltage and date. You select the class from your maximum system voltage plus the required safety margin.
The insulating rubber provides the dielectric barrier but is easily cut or punctured, which would compromise its protection. Leather over-protectors shield the rubber from mechanical damage during work. Insulating gloves must never be used for live work without their over-protectors unless the task specifically permits it under your safe-system-of-work.
IEC 60903 and most national regulations require periodic dielectric retesting — commonly every 6 months — plus a visual and air-inflation inspection before each use. We mark each glove for date tracking and can advise on, or support, a managed retest and certification program for your fleet.
Yes. Arc-flash protection addresses thermal energy, which is a different hazard from electric shock. We supply arc-rated gloves specified to the ATPV your task's incident-energy analysis requires, and can advise on integrating them with insulating gloves in a complete electrical PPE system.
