Overview

Precision-Manufactured Welding Gloves

Welding demands a glove that manages radiant and contact heat without sacrificing the feel a welder needs to lay a clean bead. Heliotrope Safety manufactures the complete welding range — supple goatskin TIG gloves for fine control, robust cowhide MIG gloves, and heavy split-leather stick gloves with full gauntlet protection and welted, heat-resistant seams.

Every welding model is built with Kevlar® thread that will not burn through at the seam, cotton or wool lining for thermal insulation, and reinforced thumb crotches where spatter concentrates. We test to EN 12477 Types A and B and EN 407 for contact and radiant heat so you can specify with confidence for any process.

FAQ

Welding Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying welding gloves at volume.

TIG requires dexterity, so we recommend a thin, unlined goatskin glove for fingertip control. Stick and high-amperage MIG generate far more heat and spatter, calling for a lined split-leather gauntlet. We supply both and can advise a matched pair-per-process program.
EN 12477 is the welding-specific standard. Type A gloves offer higher heat and mechanical protection for heavy processes; Type B prioritises dexterity for precision work like TIG. Every welding model is marked with its type on the cuff and datasheet.
Cotton or nylon thread scorches and fails at the seam long before the leather does, causing the glove to split open. 100% Kevlar® thread resists that char, so the glove stays intact through its full service life. It is standard across our welding range.
Yes. We build 16-inch welted gauntlets and aluminised-back foundry gloves for furnace and hot-metal work, tested to EN 407 for radiant heat. These are configured to order with your lining and reinforcement spec.
