Overview

Precision-Manufactured Fabric & Cotton Gloves

Not every task needs an engineered glove — and Heliotrope Safety produces the fabric and cotton range that handles the enormous everyday middle of industry. Cotton drill and jersey gloves for general handling, seamless string-knit gloves for assembly and machine work, and lint-controlled inspection gloves for quality and handling of finished goods, all manufactured at true production scale.

These gloves double as thermal and comfort liners under coated or chemical gloves, as disposable-economy handling gloves, and as branded promotional and event gloves. We knit and cut them in a full range of weights, from lightweight interlock inspection gloves to heavy terry-loop grip gloves, and offer PVC-dotted variants where a little extra grip is needed without stepping up to a full coating.
