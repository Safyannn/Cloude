Overview

Precision-Manufactured Coated Fabric Gloves

Coated fabric gloves are the volume backbone of industrial hand protection, and precision coating is a core competency at Heliotrope Safety. We knit seamless liners in nylon, polyester, cotton and HPPE across 10- to 18-gauge, then apply the exact coating the task demands — nitrile, latex, polyurethane, PVC or hybrids — on automated dipping lines that hold coating weight consistent pair to pair.

Coating chemistry is chosen for the environment: PU for dry precision, foam and sandy nitrile for oily grip, crinkle latex for wet surfaces, and full or double dips for liquid and chemical splash resistance. This is our most configurable, most scalable category — the same glove can be tuned across dozens of grip, cut and cuff combinations for a distributor's complete general-handling range.

FAQ

Coated Fabric Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying coated fabric gloves at volume.

PU suits dry precision work needing feel and low lint. Foam-nitrile is the versatile all-rounder for dry-to-lightly-oily handling. Sandy-nitrile grips oily surfaces best. Crinkle-latex excels on wet surfaces but can trigger latex allergies. We help match chemistry to your task, surface condition and any allergy constraints.
Higher gauge (15–18) means a thinner, more dexterous glove for fine work; lower gauge (10–13) means a thicker, tougher glove for heavier handling. Coating weight affects abrasion life and liquid resistance. We hold both consistent in production so every pair in a lot performs identically.
Yes. We simply knit the liner in HPPE or a steel/glass blend instead of nylon, then apply the same coating. That gives you a familiar foam-nitrile or PU grip glove with cut protection up to ANSI A6, without changing the format your crews already know.
Our double-dipped full-nitrile and PVC models offer splash and light-chemical resistance tested to EN 374. For sustained immersion in aggressive chemicals, specify the exact substances and contact times and we will recommend the correct chemistry and thickness.
