Overview

Precision-Manufactured Mechanical & Technical Gloves

The modern mechanic's glove is a technical textile product, and it is one of our highest-volume categories. Heliotrope Safety builds synthetic-leather and microfibre gloves that deliver bare-hand feel with real durability: silicone-printed grip palms, foam and gel padding at pressure points, and stretch-spandex backs that move with the hand through a full range of motion.

This range is where fine detail matters — laser-cut fingertips for touchscreen use, terry thumb-brows for wiping sweat, hook-and-loop cuffs for a secure fit, and abrasion-reinforced palms that survive months of tool handling. We tune each model between dexterity and protection so it fits the exact technical task, from delicate electronics assembly to heavy vehicle maintenance.

Materials & Construction

Built From the Yarn Up

We control construction at every stage — material selection, knitting or cutting, coating or sewing, and inspection — so performance is engineered in, not hoped for.

  • Synthetic leather (PU / microfibre) palms for consistency and durability
  • Four-way stretch spandex and nylon backs
  • Silicone and TPR grip printing at wear points
  • EVA foam and gel palm padding for vibration and comfort
  • Conductive yarn fingertips for touchscreen operation

FAQ

Mechanical & Technical Gloves — Frequently Asked

Technical and commercial answers for buyers specifying mechanical & technical gloves at volume.

Mechanic's gloves prioritise dexterity and feel — thin synthetic palms, stretch backs and articulated fingers — so the wearer can handle small fasteners and tools without removing them. General work gloves prioritise raw abrasion and cut life over fine control. We build both and will match the glove to the task.
Most synthetic and knit models can take conductive fingertips on the index and thumb, laser-cut or knitted in. It is a low-cost addition that keeps crews productive when they are scanning, using tablets or operating equipment interfaces without de-gloving.
