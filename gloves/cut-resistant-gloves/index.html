Overview

Precision-Manufactured Cut-Resistant Gloves

Cut protection is a precision engineering problem, and it is where our knitting and coating lines do their most technical work. Heliotrope Safety knits HPPE, glass-fibre and steel-core composite yarns on 13-, 15- and 18-gauge machines to deliver the full protection ladder — ANSI A2 through A9 and EN 388 cut levels B through F — without turning the glove into a board.

Coating is matched to the environment: breathable PU for dry precision handling, sandy and foam nitrile for oily grip, and full nitrile dips for liquid resistance. Every batch is tested on a TDM-100 cut tester to verify the rated level, so the A5 you order is the A5 you receive, lot after lot.
