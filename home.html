How Production Works

From Tech Pack to Export in Six Steps

Every order at BlackForgeX follows the same documented process, so buyers know exactly where their production stands at each stage.

STEP 01

Tech Pack & Specification

You share a tech pack, sample, or reference product. If you don't have a tech pack, our development team builds one with you.

STEP 02

Sampling

A physical pre-production sample is developed in 7–14 days and revised until you approve fit, fabric, and construction.

STEP 03

Fabric Sourcing & Approval

Fabrics, trims, membranes, and hardware are sourced to spec, with swatches and lab-dip approvals before cutting begins.

STEP 04

Bulk Production

Cutting, sewing, printing, and embroidery run in-house, with bulk lead times of 30–45 days after sample approval.

STEP 05

Quality Inspection

In-line checks during production and a final AQL inspection before packing, with reports available on request.

STEP 06

Packing & Export

Garments are packed to your retail or bulk specification and shipped by sea, air, or courier to 20+ countries.

FAQ

Common Questions from Buyers

Direct answers to the questions brands ask before starting production. For anything specific to your project, contact the BlackForgeX team.

The minimum order quantity at BlackForgeX starts at 100 pieces per style. Highly technical products such as fully seam-taped waterproof jackets may require higher minimums depending on fabric availability, which is confirmed at the quotation stage.

Sample development takes 7–14 working days after the tech pack and fabric details are confirmed. Complex technical outerwear may take longer if specialty fabrics or hardware need to be sourced first.

Bulk production typically takes 30–45 days after sample approval and order confirmation. Exact timelines depend on order quantity, fabric lead times, and the complexity of printing, embroidery, and finishing.

BlackForgeX exports worldwide and currently serves brands in 20+ countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Australia, Canada, and across Europe and the Middle East. Shipping is arranged by sea, air, or courier depending on order size and urgency.

With OEM manufacturing, you provide the design and tech pack, and BlackForgeX produces the garment exactly to your specification. With ODM manufacturing, BlackForgeX supplies existing development — patterns, fits, and constructions — which are then customized and branded as your own product. Both models are available on every product category.

BlackForgeX works with waterproof-breathable laminates, softshell bonded fabrics, ripstop nylon, polyester blends, fleece, down and synthetic insulation, moisture-wicking knits, and cotton blends. Fabric sourcing, custom dyeing, and lab-dip approval are handled in-house as part of every order.

Available branding options include screen printing, sublimation printing, DTF printing, embroidery, heat transfers, woven labels, printed hangtags, and custom retail packaging. All branding is applied in-house during production, so finished goods ship market-ready under your own label.

Send your tech pack, reference product, or product idea through the contact page at blackforgex.com/contact. If you don't have a tech pack yet, the development team can build one with you as the first step. You'll receive a quotation covering sampling cost, unit pricing, MOQ, and lead times.
