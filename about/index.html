About Us

Two Decades of Hand Protection

Heliotrope Safety is a vertically integrated manufacturer of industrial safety gloves, supplying distributors, PPE brands and industrial buyers across more than 60 countries from a single certified facility.

Our Story

From a Single Line to a Global Range

Heliotrope Safety began over twenty years ago with a single leather-glove line and one conviction: that the reliable way to guarantee a safety glove is to make every part of it yourself. That principle drove us to bring knitting, coating, leather cutting, sewing, finishing and testing all in-house.

Today that vertical integration spans thirteen glove categories and a 25-million-pair annual capacity, serving industries from offshore energy to cold-chain logistics. What has not changed is the conviction we started with — control the process, and you control the protection.

We are equally a factory and a development partner. A large share of our output ships under our customers' own brands, developed by our OEM/ODM team from first sample to finished, packaged, export-ready product.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles that govern how we specify, manufacture and partner.

Protection First

Every specification decision starts with the wearer's safety. We would rather lose an order than ship a glove that under-protects.

Engineered, Not Improvised

We build gloves to the hazard, standard and volume in front of us — not the nearest item already on a shelf.

True Partnership

We treat distributors and brands as long-term partners, holding specs on file and growing programs year over year.

Verifiable Quality

ISO 9001 processes, an in-house test lab and per-batch documentation mean our claims are evidenced, not asserted.

Certifications

Certified, Audited, Compliant

Our quality and social-compliance credentials are maintained through independent audit, not self-declaration.
